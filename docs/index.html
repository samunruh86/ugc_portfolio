Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about content, timelines, and how working together works.

Do you provide the product or is it sent to you?

Brands send the product so I can create real-life content using it. This allows me to film authentic demos and show how it actually fits into everyday use.

How long does it take to receive the videos?

Most videos are delivered within a few days after receiving the product. Timing may vary slightly depending on the project, but I keep turnaround fast and consistent.

Can the videos be used for ads and social media?

Yes — videos can be used across social media, Amazon listings, and ads unless discussed otherwise. I create content that’s flexible and ready to use across platforms.
